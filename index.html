MeetCueKnow what to say next

Live talking points for meetings and interviews, and a practice mode to get used to the pace. Runs on your computer with your own speech and model API keys.

Install
Five-minute tour

Meeting prompter

Press Ctrl+Alt+Space. When someone asks you a question, numbered talking points appear in about a second and a half, and tick off as you cover them.

Practice mode

Run a mock interview on your own with built-in samples, or import subtitles and question lists. MeetCue asks them aloud with real-meeting timing and gives you a review afterwards.

Latency probe

Measure how fast each speech and model provider responds from your network, compare prices, and pick the combination that suits you.

Install in one minute ​

MeetCue for Windows installs with Scoop, without administrator rights:

powershell
scoop bucket add meetcue https://github.com/Amazingkenneth/meetcue
scoop install meetcue

Then follow first-time setup.

What it looks like ​

The panel during the sample interview (MeetCue.exe --demo): the first question is answered and all its points are ticked; the second is being answered, with the next point to cover highlighted.
